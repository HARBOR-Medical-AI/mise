A ChRIS session and a stratus session each keep their own theme: ChRIS starts in MEDICAL, the cloud in LOWER DECKS.

The theme was one choice per browser, so the cloud's screens came up in whatever the ChRIS session had chosen, and the other way round. The page now paints the session's own theme once the backend has answered, remembers a press under that session's key only, and starts each session in its own look when nothing was chosen.
