The VM controller: `vm` or the VIRTUAL MACHINES tile takes the display over one machine, its script written as you go.

A pill per machine and + NEW on the gutter; the machine's lines as seats, SIZE, BUILD, CHRIS, USERS, and TEARDOWN as its own undoing; the header its facts from `vm status`, its condition, the MODE pill and the lock. A new machine is named and committed: its script is written under `/scripts/mine/` with its declaration and its build, then `vm new` is sent; a machine kept with no script gets one from its record at its first act; a CHRIS not in the script is appended by RUN. The script screen stays the library and the replay (docs/stratus.adoc, D17 revised; law a-controller-leaves-a-script).
