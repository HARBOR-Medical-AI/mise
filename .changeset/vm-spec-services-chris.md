A machine's seats are SPEC, SERVICES and CHRIS: ports and public SSH as services, a ChRIS with its accounts in one pane. SERVICES opens and closes ports with one BUILD, and public SSH for an administrative Linux user (`vm new --ssh <port> --ssh-user ops --ssh-key-file <key.pub>`, key-only, with sudo; `--ssh off` keeps the user and key), set up on the machine over the bastion by a new host step that also admits the declared ports in the machine's own firewall. CHRIS draws the line beside what runs, what a replay does, RUN and its accounts; the VM LOG is shut to its newest line until pressed; SPEC's levers take the width while a build runs. Declaring a VM again no longer marks its ChRIS changed, a replay with no recorded versions says what it does, and the tenancy's vault keeps the newer deploy record.
