The VM screens show one header band, the machines as a listing, SIZE as one pane, and a build as one visible run. The header no longer repeats the body: it names the machine, its condition, the mode, its cost now, what runs and the lock. MACHINES lists every machine with its columns and opens one on a press; the legends and the MACHINE seat are gone. SIZE puts each lever's share of the cost beside it, the whole change under them, and a BUILD that names the change and its price. A build draws its steps (plan data, plan machine, the question, apply, grow) with the time so far and its outcome, follows its task without a press, and logs to the VM LOG alone; the password that raises a READ session is asked in plain words. vm build asks plainly and once: the change, whether it reboots or grows online, the bill before and after a month, and that a NO builds nothing; the log keeps the answer, not the question again. stratus passes on only what a Resource Manager job does, never Terraform's set-up, and an empty job log no longer prints undefined.
