A machine's seats are one column: each lever is slid or typed in step, and one change block lists the changes and BUILD. SPEC's rows are a label, a slider the width of the pane, a number box in step with it (its range and step said, arrow keys stepping), what runs, and the lever's own share of the cost when it differs; under them a line a change, the month as it runs against as offered, and an ordinary BUILD naming the change or counting them. SERVICES and CHRIS list their changes the same way, their act buttons of ordinary size. An edited script line no longer says "unchanged" under it.
