An option typed on a script or VM seat stays under the hand: a space before the next word is kept. The whole seat was drawn again at each keystroke and the field rebuilt from the trimmed value, so a value with a space in it could not be typed, and whatever was being composed or undone in the field was lost. The line, its state, the column beside the form and SAVE are now brought up to date where they stand.
