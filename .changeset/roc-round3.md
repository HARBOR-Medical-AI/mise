Receipts are read after the act, and commits that write a record alone say so. A VM build or teardown re-reads the tenancy before it answers; an adopted cluster keeps what the cloud attested; a cluster torn down whole stays listed as GONE; billing starts with the billing step. DECLARE, SAVE, PULL and ADOPT stand apart from the commits that bill and say they build nothing, with their receipts in place; KILL leaves its receipt, and + NEW's BUILD waits for its price.
