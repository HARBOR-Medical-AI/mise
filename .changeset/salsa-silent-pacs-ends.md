A retrieve nobody can be asked about ends its silent series after 60 seconds, each with its reason. With no one to answer "keep waiting?", a series the PACS sent nothing of, and CUBE has none of, ends `unconfirmed` saying `the PACS sent nothing for 60 s and CUBE has none of it`; a series the watch lost sight of keeps its own words. The silence the question reports is the whole silence since the PACS last sent anything, however many times the answer was to keep waiting, so a caller that waits up to a limit reaches it.
