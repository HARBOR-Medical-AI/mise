A ChRIS's settings show BERTHS, BERTH CPU and BERTH MEMORY, edited in place like PORTER. The CHRIS and SPEC bands say how many berths the machine holds. porter reads the berth capacity rule from menu. The CHRIS band adds the count at the line's limits once they differ from what runs. porter counts the host's memory in whole GiB.
