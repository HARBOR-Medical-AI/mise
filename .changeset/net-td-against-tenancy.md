A network's range is read from its VCN, and a cluster's steps are drawn against what its compartment holds. `net status` carries `stands {cidr, at}` and `net build` answers with the VCN read back; the network seat draws the VCN's range with when. The TD controller marks each step that makes something in OCI with what the tenancy holds of it, and a step done of which it holds none as the disagreement.
