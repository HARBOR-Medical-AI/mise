Every stratus screen wears MODE READ or MANAGE; a control that would act asks your password once in READ; VIRTUAL MACHINES has its tile.

A MODE pill stands beside CONDITION on the TD controller and the script screen, and `MODE READ` on the dashboard's strip. In a READ session BUILD, ADOPT, TEAR DOWN, RUN on a build or deploy line, TEARDOWN and ADD are drawn dashed and ask the person's password first on a sheet; the daemon checks it at the door they logged in by and the session is MANAGE until the page leaves. Where the daemon has no door they read REFUSED with the reason. `stratus oci` enters as READ without a question; `stratus oci manage` asks the password in the session. The halted CONDITION reads PLANNED or PARTLY BUILT, not STOPPED. VIRTUAL MACHINES follows FILES: each VM kept here with its state, the ChRIS on it or its size and what it bills an hour; a row opens the script that declares it (docs/stratus.adoc, D14, D16, D17).
