The script screen follows a running step and never holds it, with the session's own `wait`; a hidden word is asked on an errand bar.

A step that runs long (`vm build`, `chris deploy`, a user add) is a task under `/proc/steps/`; its seat wears ▶, the log lands under the step, and STOP FOLLOWING in the header or LEAVE is the console's Ctrl-C, freeing the lane while the task goes on. A script opens on its running seat and follows it again; FOLLOW follows one the operator had stopped; the SCRIPTS tile reads RUNNING · VM BUILD meanwhile. A hidden word (a user's password, the admin's) is asked on the errand's own bar across the body, its field hidden and emptied as it is read, never on the strip and never kept (docs/stratus.adoc, D17).
