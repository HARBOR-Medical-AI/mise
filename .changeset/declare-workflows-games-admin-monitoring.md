The games, `play`, `expect`, `gather`, `user` and `proc` refuse a bad option before running, and Tab completes them.

The Workflows commands (`play`, `expect`, `record`, `gather`), the Admin command `user` (with its alias `users`), the Monitoring command `proc`, and the 49 commands of the games shelf (`fortune`, `cal`, `weather`, `motd`, the text and number toys, the sky and time toys, the lab's tools `who`, `uptime`, `say`, `ping`, `chrisfetch`, `xxd`, `strings`, `sha256sum` and `md5sum`, and the showpieces and games) are each declared in one record: grammar, argument kinds, handler, holds and help, and their help-table entries are gone. A declaration may name aliases, answered, parsed and completed as the command itself. `calc` (an arithmetic expression, `calc -3+4`) and `morse` (Morse code is dashes) take their words as they come. What changes on a line: an unknown option or a stray operand is refused with the usage before the command runs (`fortune x`, `who x`, `quiz x`, `proc here now`, `record rewind`); a word starting with `-` in the words of a text toy (`rev`, `tac`, `rot13`, `shuf`, `yes`, `figlet`, `banner`, `cowsay`, `cowthink`, `lolcat`, `qr`, `wtf`, `say`) follows `--`; a wildcard in a word that is not a path is no longer matched against CFS paths; a bad number for `-n`, `-W`, `-c`, `--limit`, `--days` or `--seed` is refused by its kind; `cal` refuses a word that is not a whole number and a third operand; `trek -f` is a switch, not counted; `units list` is the list (`units --list` is gone); `ping` takes only `cube`; `xxd` and `strings` refuse a wildcard that matches several files; `timer cancel` and `stopwatch start` take their word first; `user` with no operation, `proc unwatch` with no feed and `play` with no manifest are refused in the parser's words; `gather process` hands every word after its executable to the run (put the run's own options after `--`, as before); `gather feed` refuses an unknown word as an unexpected operand; `users` has the help page of `user`. `sudo`, answered by the shell before the registry, keeps its help page.
