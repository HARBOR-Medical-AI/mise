A ChRIS's status reads each person's berth through porter, with the admin's credentials, beside the accounts. A berth is running with its CPU and memory, or stopped with the home kept; when porter cannot say, the status says why (an older porter names its version). The live read carries `berths` and `berthsWhy`, both optional in the menu schema, so an older daemon and a newer ARGUS work together either way.
