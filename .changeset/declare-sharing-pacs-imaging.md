Tab completes `pacs`, `dcm`, `setfacl` and the other Sharing, PACS and Imaging commands; a bad line is refused.

`setfacl`, `getfacl`, `chmod`, `setfattr`, `getfattr`, `pacs`, `query`, `pull`, `pacsservers`, `pacsqueries`, `pacsretrieve`, `dcm` and `image` are each declared in one record: grammar, argument kinds, handler, holds and help. Their lines parse with GNU `getopt_long` rules and are refused in the parser's words with a usage generated from the grammar; their help pages, completion and tool schema read the same record. `pacs query` and `pacs pull` take the very lines `query` and `pull` take. `pacsservers`, `pacsqueries` and `pacsretrieve` are parsed here, then handed to chili as its own words. The ChRIS backend registers the `pacsQuery` kind: a query by id, by path under `/net/pacs/queries` or as a `Key:Value` question, completed from the session's query index. A value option may `ask`: given no value it is a question, as `query … --csv-to` always was. `help pacs query` (any declared command's subcommand) is a page generated from the subcommand's grammar. What changes on a line: a wildcard in a query's question (`PatientName:SMITH*`) is the PACS's to read and is never matched against CFS; `pull --plugin` and `--pipeline` are refused as unrecognized options rather than with the retirement note; `pull --new-feed --retry 1` takes `--retry` as the title, as getopt does; `getfacl` and `getfattr` refuse an option they do not have rather than ignoring it; `setfattr` refuses `-n` and `-x` together rather than letting the last win, refuses a missing `-n`/`-x` or `-v` on the error channel with exit status 1, and names `tag` and `user.tag` when given another attribute; `chmod` names `o+r` and `o-r` when given another mode; `pacs status` refuses an option or a second target rather than ignoring them, and `pacs connect` a second server; `dcm`, `image` and `pacs status` refuse a wildcard that matches several paths where they take one; `query --title` with no value is refused as wanting one; `pacsservers`, `pacsqueries` and `pacsretrieve` refuse an unknown option, a non-numeric id or a missing subcommand before chili runs.
