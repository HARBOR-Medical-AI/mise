The script screen renders what runs beside its VM seats and an edit as its difference against what stands. VM seats read `vm status` (RUNS, BUILD WOULD, the lock with where it is kept, undoings only for what stands); `vm protect` and `vm teardown` lines wear a standing; an unsaved edit is read as the screen's text, SAVE appears at once, and a network's line shows its range as it stands → the line's.
