The wire gains `elevate`, `vm status` gains a model, and a halted cluster reads PLANNED or PARTLY BUILT.

`elevateMessageSchema` carries a password once; `elevatedMessageSchema` says granted or why not; `attachedMessageSchema.elevation` says a door stands. `vmStatusModelSchema` (`stratus.vm.status`) is a row per VM. `CLUSTER_CONDITIONS` reads PLANNED and PARTLY BUILT where it read STOPPED (docs/stratus.adoc, D14, D16, D17).
