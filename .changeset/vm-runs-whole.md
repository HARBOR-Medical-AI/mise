What a VM runs is read whole from the tenancy: its open ports, its data's backups and its OS, beside its sizes. `vm status` and the row's `stands` carry `open`, `backup` and `os`; a build line and BUILD WOULD compare each with what is declared; SIZE draws RUNS beside the ports, backups and OS, MACHINE's OPEN is what the security group opens, and TEARDOWN's blocks say whether each part stands as the tenancy answered, with when.
