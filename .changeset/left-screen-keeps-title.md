Leaving a machine's or a script's screen gives the browser tab its own title back, and it stays.

A screen left with Esc while a read was still in flight was drawn once more when the read answered, and that drawing retitled the tab with the machine's condition (`BUILT · store`) though the page stood again. A screen that has been left no longer writes the tab's title.
