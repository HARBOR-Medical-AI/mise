On a takeover, a press on the view already shown sends the frame's gutter away, as the page's gutter goes.

The page's gutter has always gone off stage on a press on the domain already shown, handing its width to the workspace; the TD controller, the VM controller and the script screen kept their gutter of views fixed. Now the same press sends it away, the restore strip or Esc brings it back, and Esc leaves the screen only once the gutter is back.
