What stands is priced as it runs, read from the tenancy, never as it is declared. `vm cost` bare reads the tenancy first and gives each VM `runs {rate, at}`: the parts that stand, a torn-down machine billing nothing and its kept data its own. The header's STANDS NOW draws that with when it was read, the declaration on its own row when it differs; a machine's screen prices that machine, the dashboard tile bills what runs, and a cluster built here is priced from its record's compartment.
