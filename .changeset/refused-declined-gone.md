A refused line, an answered no and a torn-down thing are rendered where they happen, not left to the log. A refusal is said in its seat with why until the seat sends again; a no is said as declined; a cluster teardown refused for its unreadable volumes is kept on its record so the guard says it coming back. A VM whose machine is gone is offered only what is left to undo; PULL, each source's clone head and SPEND's answer stand in their panels with when.
