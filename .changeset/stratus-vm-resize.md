A VM that stands can be resized: `vm new` with new sizes, then `vm build`. More vCPUs or memory change the machine in place, and the question before the apply says it reboots and what goes down with it; a grown boot disk or data volume has its filesystem grown afterwards over the bastion, so the space appears. A shrink, a new OS or a replaced volume is still refused unless `--allow-alter`.
